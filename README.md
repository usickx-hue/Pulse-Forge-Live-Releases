# Pulse Forge Live

Create music videos automatically from your music and media.

Pulse Forge Live is a Windows desktop application that turns music, images and videos into ready-to-publish music videos. Add your track, choose an image or video, customize visualizers and effects, preview the result and render the finished video.

## What is Pulse Forge Live?

Pulse Forge Live is designed for creating music videos without manually editing every frame in a traditional video editor. Combine music, images or videos, visualizers, beat-reactive effects, camera movement, backgrounds and rendering presets in one workflow.

## Features

- Automatic music video creation
- Live Preview
- Audio-reactive visualizers
- Beat-synced effects
- Auto Zoom and Auto Scenes
- Particles and glow effects
- Image or video backgrounds
- Square or circular media presentation
- Separate track rendering and combined rendering
- 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 layouts
- 720p, 1080p, 1440p and 4K
- 30 FPS and 60 FPS
- Russian, Ukrainian and English interface
- Built-in updater

## How it works

1. Add your music.
2. Add an image or video.
3. Choose a visualizer.
4. Enable the effects you want.
5. Preview the result.
6. Select resolution and FPS.
7. Render the finished video.

## Who is it for?

- YouTube music channels
- Artists and producers
- Phonk, electronic and anime-style music videos
- Music visualizers
- Shorts and vertical music videos
- Anyone who wants fast music video creation without a full video editor

## Installation

1. Download **Pulse Forge Live Setup.exe**.
2. Run the installer.
3. Complete the installation.
4. Launch Pulse Forge Live.
5. Select your interface language on first launch.

The current Windows build is not digitally signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning.

## System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- 64-bit system
- GPU acceleration recommended for high-resolution rendering
- Enough free disk space for rendered videos
